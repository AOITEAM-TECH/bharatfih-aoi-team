Who we are

The last line of defense before a board leaves SMT.

Bharat FIH runs high-volume PCB assembly across several domains inside Surface Mount Technology (SMT) — the process of soldering electronic components directly onto the surface of a printed circuit board. Our team sits inside that process as the quality checkpoint: nothing moves forward without passing through us first.

We specifically own two checkpoints on the line — SPI (Solder Paste Inspection), right after paste printing, and AOI (Automatic Optical Inspection), which we run in two stages after placement and reflow. Together, these three stations catch the majority of defects before a board ever reaches final test.

01

Catch it early

SPI flags paste problems before components are even placed — the earliest possible save.

How a board moves through us

From bare paste to a passed board.

STEP 01

Solder paste printing

Solder paste is stencil-printed onto the bare board's pads — the foundation every component will sit on.

SMT line
STEP 02

SPI — Solder Paste Inspection

We measure paste volume, height, and alignment on every pad. Bad paste gets caught here, before a single component is placed.

Our team
STEP 03

Component placement

Pick-and-place machines position every component onto the pasted pads at high speed.

SMT line
STEP 04

Reflow soldering

The board passes through a reflow oven, melting the paste to permanently bond each component to the board.

SMT line
STEP 05

AOI Stage 1

Immediately after reflow, we optically scan every board for placement shifts, missing parts, tombstoning, and solder bridges.

Our team
STEP 06

AOI Stage 2 — final check

After any rework, boards return to us for a second, final optical pass before they're cleared to move downstream.

Our team
STATION 01

SPI

Solder Paste Inspection. A 3D scan of every printed pad, checking paste volume, height, and position right after stencil printing — the earliest quality gate on the line.

STATION 02

AOI Stage 1

Automatic Optical Inspection — post-reflow. A camera system scans every soldered board for missing, misaligned, or shifted components right after reflow.

STATION 03

AOI Stage 2

Automatic Optical Inspection — final. A last optical pass after rework, confirming the board is genuinely clean before it leaves our checkpoint.
